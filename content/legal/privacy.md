ESIO ApS ("ESIO", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use our services.

## Information we collect

- **Account data:** name, email, organisation name.
- **Financial data:** transactions, balances, and forecasts pulled from your connected accounting tools, used solely to power the features you've enabled.
- **Usage data:** anonymous events about how the product is used, to help us prioritise improvements.

## How we use it

We use your data only to deliver the service you've signed up for. We do not sell your data, ever, to anyone, for any reason.

## Your rights under GDPR

You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your data at any time. Email **privacy@esio.dk** and we'll respond within 30 days.
