ESIO is fully compliant with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). This page explains your rights and how to exercise them.

## Your rights

- **Access:** request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
- **Rectification:** correct inaccurate data.
- **Erasure:** delete your account and associated data.
- **Portability:** export your data in a machine-readable format.
- **Restriction:** limit how we process your data.
- **Objection:** object to specific processing activities.

## Exercising your rights

Email **gdpr@esio.dk** with your request. We respond within 30 days as required by law.
