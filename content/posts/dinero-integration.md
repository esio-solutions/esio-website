We've shipped first-party Dinero integration. If your accounting lives in Dinero, you can now connect it from **Settings → Integrations** and ESIO will pull transactions and balances automatically — no CSV exports, no copy-paste.

## What gets synced

- Transactions (income + expenses), with category mapping
- Account balances, refreshed every 4 hours
- VAT obligations, derived from Dinero's tax codes
- Customer and supplier ledgers (read-only)

## What doesn't

We don't write back to Dinero. ESIO is read-only on your accounting data — your accountant remains the single source of truth, ESIO just makes the future legible.
