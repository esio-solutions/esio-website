We built ESIO because the founders we worked with kept asking the same question: *"is this a good month, or just a busy one?"*

Spreadsheets answer that question, eventually, after an evening of work. Accounting tools answer different questions entirely. ESIO closes the gap: real-time cashflow, tax forecasting, and clear "what does this mean?" insights — built for people who'd rather be running their business than reconciling rows.

## What's in the v1

- **Liquidity Forecasting** — daily cash projection with scenario sliders
- **Tax Helper** — quarterly/annual obligations tracked automatically
- **Accounting integration** — first-party connectors for E-conomic and Dinero
- **Budget tracker** — set targets, see variance, drill in when something looks off
