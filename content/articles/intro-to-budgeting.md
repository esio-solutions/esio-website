Most founders treat budgeting as a chore that happens once a year, after which the budget gets filed and ignored. That's how surprises happen — surprise tax bills, surprise cashflow gaps, surprise quarters where [revenue is fine but the bank balance isn't](/articles/cashflow-vs-profit/).

## What a budget is actually for

A budget isn't a forecast. It's a *constraint* — a statement of what you intend to spend, against which you measure what you actually spent. The gap between the two is where every learning happens.

When you treat your budget as a living document — updated weekly, adjusted as reality shifts — it becomes the most useful management tool in the business.
