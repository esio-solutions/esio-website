It's the most counter-intuitive lesson in small-business finance: a *profitable* business can run out of money. Profit lives in your books. Cash lives in your bank account. They are not the same thing, and the gap between them has killed more companies than any market downturn.

## A simple example

You invoice a client EUR 50,000 on March 1st. Your books show EUR 50,000 of revenue. Your accountant nods. Your tax obligation accrues. The client pays on June 30th. For four months, you owed payroll, rent, and suppliers — all out of cash you didn't yet have.

Profit said you were up. Cash said you were drowning.

## What to actually watch

- **Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)** — how long money is tied up in unpaid invoices.
- **Cash runway** — months you can survive at current burn before income arrives.
- **Tax provisioning** — money you owe but haven't paid yet.
