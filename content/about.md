ESIO is a budgeting app built specifically for micro-companies — sole traders, freelancers, family-run cafés, and 2–5 person teams. The kind of business where the founder is also the bookkeeper, and where last quarter's reconciliation always happens too late.

## Why ESIO exists

Most accounting software answers backward-looking questions: *"what happened last month?"* Most spreadsheets answer them with effort. Neither answers the question that actually keeps founders up at night: *"what's my bank balance going to look like in three months?"*

ESIO closes that gap. Real-time cashflow projections, tax obligations tracked automatically, scenario modelling for the decisions that matter — all built around the constraint that you don't have an accountant on staff and you don't want to become one.

## What we believe

- **The future should be legible.** If you can't read what's coming in your business, you're guessing. We make the next 18 months as readable as last week's bank statement.
- **Boring is the goal.** Surprises in small-business finance are bad surprises. Good admin is the *absence* of drama, not the presence of complex tools.
- **Micro-companies deserve software that fits them.** Enterprise BI tools and personal finance apps both miss this market. We don't.
