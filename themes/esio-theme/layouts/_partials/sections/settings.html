{{- /* Theme + language picker content. Rendered inside the nav burger panel (see sections/nav.html). Theme keys are camelCase in TOML (`modernPro`) but rendered kebab-case in the data-theme attribute (`modern-pro`); assets/js/shared/theme.js handles the swap on click and persists the choice to localStorage.
